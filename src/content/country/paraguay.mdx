## 📝 Notes

### 🌍 Why Visit Paraguay?
Paraguay is worth considering for its local character rather than a long checklist of sights. International tourism is limited, prices are relatively low, and daily life is less oriented toward visitors than in many neighboring destinations.
Guaraní is widely spoken alongside Spanish, and traditions remain part of everyday life. The country is a good fit for a short, low-key cultural stop, particularly alongside Argentina.

### 🌤️ Climate
**May–August** is generally the most comfortable period for city walks, with cooler temperatures and occasional cold fronts.  
**November–March** is often very hot and humid; spring and autumn are variable.

### ⚠️ Safety and Travel Conditions
Paraguay is less polished than Uruguay or Chile, but the main visitor areas are generally manageable. Infrastructure can be inconsistent, and caution is especially important in some urban neighborhoods and northern or eastern border regions. The limitations are more about uneven services and a lack of tourism infrastructure than difficulty reaching the main cities.
