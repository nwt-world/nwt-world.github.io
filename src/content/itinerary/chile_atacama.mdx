## Overview

San Pedro de Atacama is a practical single base for exploring the Chilean Altiplano.

The route is structured by altitude.
Volcanoes are one of the defining features of the region. They are visible directly from San Pedro
and remain part of the landscape on almost every excursion.

**Region:** Antofagasta, Northern Chile  
**Base:** San Pedro de Atacama  
**Overall time:** 4–6 days for the core Atacama route; +3–4 days for Uyuni; +1 day for a summit  
**Main focus:** volcano views, salt lagoons, Altiplano landscapes, geysers, desert scenery  
**Best season:** April–June and September–November; winter is dry but cold, while summer can bring highland rain

## Tours or rental car

The core Chilean Atacama route can be done either with organized tours or with a rented car.

Tours are usually more practical for 1–2 travelers because they:
- handle attraction tickets, check-in times, and local access rules
- provide transport, and often breakfast / lunch / water on remote full-day routes
- remove the need to drive before sunrise or above 4,000 m
- run frequently from San Pedro

A rental car gives more timing flexibility and can become economical for a larger group.
The main highways around San Pedro are good, but some attraction approaches are unpaved and high-altitude sites can require advance reservations or specific check-in procedures.

## Best season

April–June:
- Stable weather after the summer highland-rain period
- Good visibility
- Increasingly cold nights toward winter

September–November:
- Dry conditions
- Less severe cold than midwinter
- Good overall balance for the full itinerary

June–August:
- Very dry
- Cold nights and especially cold El Tatio mornings

December–March:
- Warmer
- Highland rain can affect high-altitude roads, views, and tour operation

San Pedro and the main attractions can be visited year-round; the main difference is cold versus highland-rain risk.
