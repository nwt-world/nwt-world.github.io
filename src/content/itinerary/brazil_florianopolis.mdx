## Overview

Florianópolis is a large island with ocean beaches, dunes, lagoon-side villages, and coastal hiking trails.

**State:** Santa Catarina  
**Overall time:** about 7 days; easily extended to 10–14 days  
**Main focus:** beaches, coastal hiking, boat trips  
**Best season:** December–March for swimming; March usually has fewer visitors than January–February. November and April are quieter but less reliable for beach weather.  

### Where to stay

Barra da Lagoa — recommended for an active week without a car:  
• Beach, fishing village, restaurants, small shops, and apartments within walking distance  
• Useful bus connections through the Lagoa da Conceição transport hub  
• Easy access to short coastal walks; trips to the far south take longer  

Campeche — good for beaches and the southern routes:  
• Wide choice of houses and apartments near the ocean  
• Convenient for the airport, Armação, and the southern hiking areas  
• The neighborhood is spread out; check the walking distance from accommodation to the beach and a bus stop

Canasvieiras / Jurerê — mainly for longer stays focused on the north:  
• Extensive accommodation, groceries, restaurants, and other everyday services  
• Generally more sheltered water than the ocean-facing east; convenient for repeated beach days nearby  
• Poor base for a short trip covering the east and south because of long cross-island journeys

## Transportation

Public buses:
- Consórcio Fênix operates an extensive network. Buses serve the eastern and southern beach districts with useful frequencies on many routes.
- The network uses transfer terminals such as TILAG (Lagoa da Conceição) and TIRIO (southern routes). Check the actual combination and departure time in the route planner.
- Since January 2026, cash is not accepted when boarding. Buy a QR ticket in the Floripa no Ponto app or use a rechargeable Tourist card; cash payment is still possible at terminal ticket offices. The Tourist card supports timed transfers under the operator's rules.

Uber / taxi:
- Useful for airport transfers and short trips between beaches; check pickup availability before relying on one from a remote trailhead.

Rental car:
- Useful for visiting several distant locations, but offers little advantage when the road is congested. Parking also takes time near busy beaches.

Traffic bottlenecks:
- SC-401, the main access road to Canasvieiras and Jurerê, is a major summer bottleneck, especially during afternoon returns from the northern beaches.
- SC-406 around Lagoa da Conceição / Praia Mole also slows down on busy beach days; SC-405 toward the south can be congested at peak times.
- These delays affect buses and cars alike. Group nearby beaches on the same excursion rather than crossing the island repeatedly.
