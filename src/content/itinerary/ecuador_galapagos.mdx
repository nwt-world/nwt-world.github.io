## Overview

The Galápagos Islands are primarily a wildlife destination, not a conventional tropical beach holiday.
Sea lions, marine iguanas, giant tortoises, penguins, seabirds, and marine life can be observed at close range,
often in habitats found nowhere else. Volcanic coastlines, lava fields, and snorkeling are the other main reasons to visit.

This is a land-based itinerary using hotels, public inter-island speedboats, and selected shared tours instead of an expensive multi-day cruise.
Start on San Cristóbal, cross to Santa Cruz, and fly home from Baltra; add Isabela if time allows.

**Region:** Galápagos, Ecuador  
**Overall time:** 6–7 days (6 nights) for San Cristóbal and Santa Cruz; add 2–3 nights for Isabela  
**Main focus:** wildlife, snorkeling, volcanic landscapes, island walks  
**Best season:** December–May for warmer water and generally calmer seas; June–November is cooler with rougher crossings

## Entry requirements and fixed costs

Before the flight from Quito or Guayaquil:
- Complete the Galápagos Transit Control Card (TCT) registration; the current tourist fee is US$20
- Complete the ABG biosecurity declaration and have baggage inspected to protect the islands from invasive species
- Keep your passport, Galápagos flight tickets, and accommodation details accessible for the entry checks

On arrival at SCY or GPS:
- Pay the Galápagos National Park entrance fee, normally in US dollars; carry sufficient cash even if card payment is sometimes available
- Standard international adult rate: US$200
- Reduced adult rate for nationals of CAN / MERCOSUR countries: US$100

---

## Budget and bookings

For a more affordable land-based trip:
- Choose simple hotels or guesthouses in Puerto Baquerizo Moreno and Puerto Ayora rather than liveaboard cruises
- Walk to the town beaches, viewpoints, and nearby visitor sites when permitted
- Reserve one worthwhile guided boat tour on each main island rather than paying for an excursion every day
- Use the regular inter-island boats; compare local agency fares with online reseller prices
- Keep cash for smaller transfers, island fees, and businesses that do not accept cards

Book earlier:
- Open-jaw flights (SCY arrival / GPS departure), especially for fixed dates
- Inter-island ferry on the date that changes your island base
- The specific high-priority boat tour if its departure days are limited
- Isabela accommodation and transfers if adding that island

A short land-based stay still carries substantial fixed entry costs; the main savings come from accommodation and the number of paid day tours.

---

## Best season

December–May:
- Warmer air and water, generally better conditions for beach time and snorkeling
- Short tropical showers are possible
- Inter-island crossings are often calmer

June–November:
- Cooler water and more cloud / garúa conditions
- Rich marine activity, including increased chances of some cold-water species
- Seas can be rougher, particularly on small inter-island speedboats

Wildlife can be observed year-round; the main planning difference is sea comfort and the type of snorkeling conditions you prefer.
