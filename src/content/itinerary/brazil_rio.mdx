## Overview

**Overall time:** 4–6 days  
**Main focus:** viewpoints, beaches, urban nature  
**Best season:** May–October for cooler and generally drier weather  
**Hot / wetter season:** December–March  

---

## Where to stay

### Ipanema

Best overall choice.

Why:  
• Direct access to Ipanema Beach and Arpoador  
• Metro stations at General Osório and Nossa Senhora da Paz  
• Easy access to Leblon, Copacabana, Jardim Botânico, and Lagoa  
• Good area for walking, restaurants, and evenings  

### Leblon

Best for a quieter stay.

Why:  
• Continuation of Ipanema Beach  
• Less tourist traffic than Copacabana  
• Metro stations at Jardim de Alah and Antero de Quental  
• Good restaurants and evening infrastructure  

Usually more expensive than Ipanema.

### Copacabana

Best for accommodation choice and transport.

Why:  
• Large number of hotels and apartments  
• Several metro stations  
• Convenient for the official Corcovado van from Praça do Lido  
• Easy access to Sugarloaf and Ipanema  

More crowded and less calm than Ipanema / Leblon.

### Botafogo

Useful alternative if beaches are secondary.

Why:  
• Excellent metro connections  
• Close to Sugarloaf  
• Good views over Botafogo Bay

## Transportation

No rental car is needed.

Metro:
- Very useful between Ipanema, Leblon, Copacabana, Botafogo, Flamengo, and Centro
- Lines 1 and 4 cover most areas relevant to this itinerary
- Contactless / local transit cards and normal metro tickets are available

For Christ:
- Trem do Corcovado from Cosme Velho, or
- official Paineiras vans from Copacabana / Largo do Machado

For Jardim Botânico and Urca:
- Uber / taxi usually saves enough time to be worthwhile

---

## What to book in advance

Christ the Redeemer:
- Book Trem do Corcovado if using the train
- Choose a timed departure
- In high season, do not rely on buying a convenient morning slot on arrival

Sugarloaf:
- Advance purchase recommended for sunset, weekends, and holidays
