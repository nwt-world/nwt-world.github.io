## Overview

**Overall time:** 4–6 days  
**Best season:** May–October for cooler, generally drier weather and outdoor sightseeing. December–March is hotter and rainier, but better suited to a beach-focused stay.

---

## Where to stay

Choose one base in Zona Sul for the entire trip.

### Ipanema

Recommended for a first visit.

- Direct access to the ocean, restaurants, cafés, and supermarkets within walking distance
- Metro: General Osório and Nossa Senhora da Paz

### Leblon

- Quieter residential area with good evening infrastructure and beachfront accommodation
- Usually more expensive than Ipanema
- Metro: Jardim de Alah and Antero de Quental

### Copacabana

- Wider choice of hotels and budget apartments, but greater crowds and street activity
- Metro: Cardeal Arcoverde, Siqueira Campos, and Cantagalo

### Botafogo

- Worth considering if accommodation prices are more important than direct access to an ocean beach
- Bay views and convenient connections towards Centro and Urca
- Metro: Botafogo

## Transportation

- Airport transfers: Uber / taxi from either Galeão (GIG) or Santos Dumont (SDU). SDU is closer to Zona Sul; allow extra time for journeys to or from GIG during rush hour.
- Metro: Lines 1 and 4 connect the main southern neighborhoods and Centro. Pay at the gate with a contactless Visa, Mastercard, or Elo card or a compatible mobile wallet; single-trip tickets are available at stations.
- Renting a car adds little value for a short stay because of traffic, parking, and the existing public transport network.
