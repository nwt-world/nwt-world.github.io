## Overview

Iguazú is a compact trip built around two different views of the same waterfall system.

The Argentina side is the longer park day, with multiple walkways and close-up views.
The Brazil side is shorter and gives the best panorama across the falls.

Puerto Iguazú is the easiest base if arriving in Argentina.
Foz do Iguaçu is optional and becomes more useful if you want Parque das Aves, Itaipu, or a Paraguay extension.

**Province:** Misiones  
**Overall time:** 2–3 nights; add 1 night for Paraguay or a slower Foz stay  
**Main focus:** waterfalls, rainforest, Triple Frontier  
**Best season:** March–May and August–October; the falls can be visited year-round

## Transportation

Puerto Iguazú → Argentina falls:
- Río Uruguay local bus
- Taxi / transfer

Puerto Iguazú → Brazil falls:
- Organized half-day tour / transfer
- Direct cross-border bus when current schedules allow
- International bus to Foz + local bus 120 to the park

Puerto Iguazú → Foz:
- Río Uruguay
- Crucero del Norte
- Other cross-border operators may run depending on the current timetable

Foz → Brazil falls:
- Bus 120 from TTU
- Uber / taxi

Puerto Iguazú → Paraguay:
- Organized tour
- Ferry to Presidente Franco
- Or travel through Foz and cross to Ciudad del Este
