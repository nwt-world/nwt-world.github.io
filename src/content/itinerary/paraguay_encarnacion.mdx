## Overview

A short overland route from Posadas in Argentina through Encarnación to Asunción.
The main cultural highlights are the Jesuit missions of Trinidad and Jesús; the cities add riverfront walks and a few historical sights.

**Overall time:** 6–7 days (about 1 night in Posadas, 2–3 in Encarnación, 2–3 in Asunción)  
**Main focus:** Jesuit heritage, Paraná River, city walks  
**Best season:** May–September; November–March is often very hot and humid
